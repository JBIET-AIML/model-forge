MODEL FORGE 2026 – Machine Learning Hackathon

This page shows the official event poster and a quick summary of the key information for participants.

Event Snapshot

  • Title: MODEL FORGE 2026 – Machine Learning Hackathon
  • Date & Time: 25 February 2026, 9:30 AM
  • Venue: JBIET Main Block
  • Team Size: 3–4 members per team
  • Registration Fee: ₹100 per head

Tracks & Focus Areas

As hinted in the poster and main site, teams can choose from multiple ML domains:

  • Natural Language Processing – text understanding, classification, and generation.
  • Time Series & Forecasting – predictive models on temporal data.
  • Computer Vision – image understanding, detection, and recognition.
  • Generative AI – creative models for text, images, and more.

What the Poster Emphasizes

The poster highlights that MODEL FORGE 2026 is not just a coding contest, but an end‑to‑end ML experience:

  • Students build models from scratch on given datasets.
  • Emphasis on real‑world problem solving and experimentation.
  • Opportunities to learn, collaborate, and showcase applied AI skills.
  • Exciting prizes, certificates, and recognition for top teams.
