Event Timeline

Follow the key milestones of MODEL FORGE 2026 from registration to prize distribution.

Phase 01

Registrations Open

Early February 2026

Registration link goes live and teams can officially enroll for the hackathon.

Mid February 2026

Last date to register teams and confirm participation.

Hackathon Day

Same Day – Afternoon

Judging & Presentations

Phase 05

Results & Prizes

Winners and runners-up are announced and felicitated.

Certificates and goodies are distributed to winners and all eligible participants.
